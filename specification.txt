HEALTHCARE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

VERSION 1.0 – INITIAL SPECIFICATION

1. Patients can register and log in to the system.
2. Patients can update their personal details.
3. Receptionists can register new patients.
4. Administrators can add, update, and delete doctor details.
5. Patients can book appointments with available doctors.
6. Doctors can view their appointment schedules.
7. Doctors can maintain basic patient medical records.
8. The system can generate basic patient and appointment reports.
